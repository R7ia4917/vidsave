Privacy Policy

This policy explains what VidSave handles, what it does not collect, how Google Play features are involved, and the privacy choices available to you.

1. Who is responsible

VidSave is an Android application operated by an independent developer (“VidSave”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). For questions about this policy or privacy-related requests, contact smartbrowser.app@gmail.com.

VidSave is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any third-party website, social network, media platform, or brand whose links or content may be processed through the app. All third-party names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

2. The short version

  • VidSave does not require a VidSave account.
  • VidSave does not include advertising and does not use AdMob or another advertising SDK.
  • VidSave does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or a Firebase backend.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • Media or files you save through the app are stored on your device; VidSave does not intentionally upload a copy to us.
  • The app uses Google Play’s In-App Updates and In-App Review features. These features are provided by Google Play and may process limited information as described below.
  • If you email us, we receive the information contained in your email so we can respond.

3. Scope and information we do not collect

This policy applies to the VidSave Android app and to this legal website. “Personal information” means information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

VidSave does not intentionally collect financial information, payment card details, government-issued identifiers, precise GPS location, contacts, calendar entries, SMS messages, call logs, microphone recordings, camera recordings, biometric data, health data, or an advertising identifier for our own purposes.

VidSave also does not create a server-side user profile, track your activity across unrelated apps, or use data for targeted advertising.

4. Information and data handling

4.1 Information you provide to us

Email correspondence. If you contact us at smartbrowser.app@gmail.com, your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide are processed so we can respond, provide support, handle legal requests, or protect the app and our rights.

4.2 Google Play In-App Updates

VidSave uses the Google Play In-App Updates feature to check whether a newer version of the app is available and, when appropriate, let Google Play present an update flow. Google states that the Play Core update feature may process device metadata, the installed application version, and information about installed modules or asset packs to determine update availability and expected update size. Google states that this data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties through this feature, and is deleted after a fixed retention period.

This processing is performed by Google Play as part of the Play Store environment and is governed by Google’s terms and privacy practices. VidSave does not use this feature to build a profile about you.

4.3 Google Play In-App Review

VidSave may ask Google Play to display its In-App Review interface. If you choose to submit a rating or written review, that information is sent to Google Play. A review may be displayed publicly on the Play Store; reviews submitted on certain test tracks may instead be visible privately to the developer. Google provides controls in your Google Play or Google Account for managing or deleting reviews.

VidSave does not receive your Google Account password or use the review feature for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4.4 Links and content processed through VidSave

If you enter, paste, share, or open a link in VidSave, your device may connect to the website or service that hosts that link in order to retrieve information or content. Those third-party services may receive standard network information such as your IP address, user agent, request headers, cookies they previously set, or other information normally sent when a device contacts a website. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

VidSave does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or platforms. You should review their terms and privacy policies before using their services.

4.5 Files saved on your device

Files saved through VidSave are written to storage available to the app or to a location you select or authorize on your Android device. We do not intentionally receive a copy of those files. Files remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the app where applicable, or Android removes app-specific storage.

4.6 Website hosting

This legal site is designed without advertising, analytics pixels, or third-party tracking scripts. If it is hosted with GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary network and server information needed to deliver the website under GitHub’s own privacy practices.

5. How long information is kept

  • Saved media/files: retained on your device for as long as you keep them.
  • App-local preferences or history, if any: retained on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android storage behavior.
  • Google Play update/review data: retained according to Google Play’s applicable policies and controls.
  • Support or legal emails: kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share personal information with advertising networks because VidSave does not contain ads.

Information may be processed by Google when you use Google Play In-App Updates or In-App Review, as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, our legal rights, or the security of VidSave.

7. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about personal information we hold, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, or request other rights provided by applicable law.

Because VidSave does not maintain user accounts or an analytics profile, we may hold little or no information that can be linked back to you. If you previously emailed us, you can request deletion of eligible correspondence by contacting smartbrowser.app@gmail.com. You can manage Play Store reviews through Google Play or your Google Account.

8. Security

VidSave is designed to minimize collection of personal information. Network communication used by Android, Google Play, or third-party content providers may use their own security mechanisms. No software or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the limited information we handle.

If you believe you have found a security issue in VidSave, contact smartbrowser.app@gmail.com with the subject “VidSave Security Report”.

9. Children

VidSave is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account, we generally do not know a user’s age. Parents or guardians who believe that we hold personal information relating to a child may contact us and request appropriate action.

10. International processing

Google and other third-party services you choose to access may operate infrastructure in multiple countries. Their processing may therefore occur outside your country of residence and is governed by their applicable terms, privacy policies, and legal safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VidSave changes, when a service provider changes, or when legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when the policy is materially changed.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, security concerns, or general legal inquiries about this policy, contact smartbrowser.app@gmail.com.
