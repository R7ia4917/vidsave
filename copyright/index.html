Copyright Policy

VidSave respects copyright and other intellectual-property rights. This page explains how to report material that you believe infringes your rights.

1. Scope of this policy

VidSave is a software tool that users control. VidSave does not claim ownership of third-party videos, images, audio, or other media merely because a user processes a link or saves a file using the app.

VidSave is designed so that saved files are stored on the user’s own device rather than being published or hosted by VidSave. If your complaint concerns content hosted on a third-party website or platform, that platform is generally in the best position to remove or restrict the source content because it controls the hosting account and the original file.

This policy is a good-faith process for copyright concerns involving material that is actually under our control, including the VidSave app itself, its branding, store-listing materials, official screenshots, and pages we operate.

2. How to send a copyright notice

If you believe material under VidSave’s control infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to enforce, email smartbrowser.app@gmail.com with the subject “Copyright Notice – VidSave” and include:

  1. Your full legal name and reliable contact information.
  2. A clear description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  3. The specific location of the allegedly infringing material under VidSave’s control, such as a URL, screenshot, store-listing element, icon, image, or feature description.
  4. An explanation of why you believe the identified use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or applicable law.
  5. A statement that the information you provided is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Please provide enough information for us to understand and investigate the complaint. Incomplete notices may delay our response.

3. What we can and cannot do

If the complaint concerns an asset, page, screenshot, icon, text, or other material controlled by VidSave, we may investigate and, when appropriate, remove, replace, disable, or otherwise address the material.

If the complaint concerns a video, image, audio file, post, or account hosted by another website, VidSave normally cannot remove the source content. In that situation, you should contact the website or platform hosting the material and use its copyright-reporting process.

We may request additional information when reasonably necessary to verify a claim, understand the location of the material, or determine what action is appropriate.

4. Counter-notices and mistakes

If material under our control was removed or restricted because of a mistake or misidentification, the affected person may contact smartbrowser.app@gmail.com with the subject “Copyright Counter-Notice – VidSave”. The message should identify the affected material, explain why the restriction is believed to be a mistake, and provide reliable contact information.

Where a specific statutory counter-notice procedure applies, we may request the additional statements or information required by that law before processing the request.

5. Repeated infringement or misuse

VidSave is intended for lawful use. We may restrict, modify, or discontinue functionality that is being used in a way that repeatedly facilitates infringement or creates legal, security, or abuse risks within our reasonable technical control.

6. Trademark and brand concerns

If you believe VidSave’s own branding, store listing, screenshots, or official pages misuse your trademark or create a likelihood of confusion, contact smartbrowser.app@gmail.com with the subject “Trademark Concern – VidSave” and describe the issue, the mark you own or represent, and the exact location of the material.

7. Other legal questions

For copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property questions, contact smartbrowser.app@gmail.com.
